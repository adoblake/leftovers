# Supplied LEFTOVERS branding

The site incorporates the user's `Leftovers Branding.zip` as artwork, not as instructions. No new finalized logo was invented.

- `public/branding/leftovers-green-gold.png` is a byte-for-byte copy of `IMAGES/Leftovers - 2 Color - 20.png`. It carries the supplied leaf L, wordmark, and “Never ending value.” line. The original raster artwork has not been redrawn or retouched.
- `components/BrandMark.tsx` and `app/icon.svg` reuse the exact two vector paths from page 1 of `BRANDING/Leftovers - Branding Layout.ai`, a PDF-compatible Illustrator document exported with `pdftocairo`. Web viewports and the favicon background are presentation details; the mark's geometry is unchanged.
- “Finding Value Everywhere.” remains the primary site tagline from the user's brief. The supplied artwork's secondary line appears within the original artwork.
- The original forest, gold, cream, and slate palette continues throughout the interface. Artwork is presented without declaring it a final approved identity.

Original artwork SHA-256: `8af63abb1e500c703644e954696a2f78c95ad4cad9620b66958609e2d128c5aa`.

Source artwork is included for this project's use as requested by its owner. Do not treat the application package's dependency licenses as a separate license for the supplied branding.
